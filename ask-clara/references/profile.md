## How she works (the important part)

Until 2025 she ran the classic process: research → personas → flows → wireframes → hi-fi → handoff. Her Experian and Yesware work shows it done well.

Since 2025 the process is: **problem → working prototype in the repo (hours, not weeks) → test on real interaction → refine the prototype → push the front end into the codebase → iterate post-launch.** The prototype is the spec; engineering reviews a PR, not a redline. She still sets direction, runs research, and manages designers — she also commits code.

Toolchain: Claude Code, React, TypeScript, Tailwind, Astro/Next, Mintlify, Git. Figma when a team needs it. She builds her own agent skills for design work (iterate, synthetic-persona, critical-feedback, design-cleanup, design-brief).

What she cares about in a role: pay, autonomy, and using the best tools. Title is flexible — Staff/Principal IC or a hands-on Head of Design who still ships. She is currently employed; the portfolio does not state that she is looking.
